✦ Our Story

From Passion
to Craft.

Ava Bakes began with a simple fascination for traditional baking. What started as a childhood mentorship under my grandmother quickly evolved into a dedicated pursuit of artisanal bread and pastry making.

Today, Ava Bakes specializes in small-batch, handcrafted sourdough baked goods produced entirely from scratch. Every item is made in a home kitchen using high-quality, whole ingredients — free from shortcuts or artificial additives.

Built on a foundation of patience and precision, Ava Bakes is dedicated to bringing authentic, time-honored baking techniques directly to the local community.

"Baking taught me that patience makes everything better — in the kitchen and in life."
